Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Dungeon Radio is a soundboard for tabletop games, made by {{developer}} (“we”). This policy covers the Dungeon Radio apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the Live Session relay server they use.

In short: we don’t have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your sounds and settings stay on your device. When you join a Live Session, what you share goes to the other people in that session and nowhere else. We don’t sell or share anyone’s data.

What stays on your device

Everything you make in the app is stored only on your device:

  • your sound library: sounds you import or record, their names, tags and settings;
  • bashes, scene kits, bookmarks, playlists and ambience;
  • your settings: theme, volume, dice colour and so on;
  • the name and picture you use in Live Sessions.

We never receive any of it. If your phone backs up apps to iCloud or Google, your backup may include it, under Apple’s or Google’s privacy terms. Deleting a sound in the app removes its file. Deleting the app removes everything.

The microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you record a sound, and only after you allow it. Recordings are saved to your sound library on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent at any other time.

Live Sessions

In a Live Session, one person (the broadcaster) plays sounds on everyone else’s devices (the listeners). The people in a session see:

  • From a listener: the name they type, the picture they choose (optional), the kind of device (“iPhone”, “Android”), their dice rolls and colour, their game answers, and any of their own sounds they play for everyone.
  • From the broadcaster: the session’s name, the sounds and ambience they play, handouts (pictures), dice rolls, roll requests and games.

Sound files and pictures are copied to the listeners’ devices so they can play and show them. Copies of sounds are deleted after 30 days without use. Handouts are deleted when the session ends.

At the table (same Wi-Fi): devices talk to each other directly over your network. The session’s name is announced on that network so listeners can find it. Nothing goes through us.

Online (with a code): messages pass through our relay server, which forwards them between the people in the session.

  • The relay keeps nothing once a session ends.
  • It doesn’t store or log names, pictures, sounds or messages. Its log records only that a session code opened or ended.
  • It runs on Render, whose network may keep standard connection logs (like IP addresses) for security and operations.

Only share sounds, pictures and names you’re happy for the others in your session to have. The broadcaster can remove anyone from a session.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app asks the store whether you own Premium, and remembers the answer on your device.

Children

Dungeon Radio isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone. If you’re a parent and think your child has shared something through a Live Session, the broadcaster can remove them. You can also contact us.

Your choices and rights

All your data is on your own device, so you can see, change and delete it at any time in the app, or delete the app. We don’t hold any of it, so we have nothing to send you, correct or delete. That includes requests under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law. If you have a question, or think we hold something about you, contact us and we’ll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what the app collects, we’ll say so in the app’s update notes.
