# Legal and store compliance

Where Dungeon Radio stands on the usual "don't get sued" checklist, for the
iPhone/iPad and Android apps sold in the stores. The Mac app is for personal
use only and isn't covered here.

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Licenses are served by the Live Session
relay. Their sources are in `live-relay/legal/`:

- https://soundboard-r1zt.onrender.com/privacy
- https://soundboard-r1zt.onrender.com/terms
- https://soundboard-r1zt.onrender.com/licenses

Fill in `live-relay/legal/details.json` (name, contact email, jurisdiction)
and redeploy the relay before publishing.

## ⚠️ The big one: the YouTube clipper on iPhone and iPad

The iPhone/iPad app includes the "Online" YouTube browser. It saves audio from
YouTube videos and blocks YouTube's ads. That is the biggest legal and review
risk in the app:

- **YouTube's Terms of Service** forbid downloading content without
  permission and forbid blocking ads.
- **App Store guideline 5.2.3** rejects apps that save audio or video from
  third-party sources like YouTube without authorization.
- **A web browser inside the app** pushes the App Store age rating to 18+
  ("Unrestricted Web Access").

**Recommendation:** leave it out of the App Store build. Keep it in the Mac app
(personal use) and, if you like, in your own test builds. It isn't on Android.

## Store questionnaires

- **Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label"):** *Data Not Collected.* Live
  Session messages pass through the relay in real time and aren't stored,
  which Apple doesn't count as collection.
- **Google Play Data safety:** *No data collected or shared.* Same reason: the
  relay processes messages only in transit. Purchases are handled by Google
  Play itself.
- **Age rating:** a soundboard and dice with no violence content of its own.
  People can share pictures and names in private, code-only sessions. The
  broadcaster can remove anyone, and the Terms ban abusive content.
  - Answer "user-generated content: yes, shared privately".
  - Expect about 12+ on the App Store, or 18+ if the YouTube browser stays in.
  - Expect "Teen" on Google Play.
- **Apple's rules for user-generated content (1.2):** sessions are private
  groups joined with a code, and the broadcaster can remove people. If review
  asks for more, the usual answer is a "Report" option. Add it then.

*This isn't legal advice. For a paid app it's worth a short review by a lawyer,
especially the Terms' liability and governing-law sections.*
